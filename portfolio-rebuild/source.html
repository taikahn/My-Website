Case Study: Using AI Tools for Live User Interfaces

As an educational experiment I have setup an AI enabled workflow inside Cursor/VSCode via Claude, Gemini, Opus, etc to enable faster interface design directly in React. The ability to focus on the mobile and desktop layouts, the breakpoints, the grids, the hovers, the menus, the navigation and all of their interactions; without resorting to detailed Figma prototyping and screenshots has been very helpful and quite eye-opening. The ability for designers to do this themselves and hand it to dev/engineering which can immediately both understand and take control of the code is a monumental shift in design workflows that are well worth taking the time to explore, use and learn.
