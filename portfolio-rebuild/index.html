About

Adaptive, experienced, and deeply comfortable with complex products

I am a highly adaptive and experienced creative whose career began at Apple, where I helped implement designs using code into products like iTunes and Mac OS X. At Ping Identity, I designed the interface for and provided front-end code for an industry-first GUI of PingFederate, their flagship identity product.

Over the last five years at Strata Identity, I designed the interface for their cloud-based Maverics Identity Orchestration Platform in Figma, helping progress the product from wireframes through hi-fi pages and ultimately into a live customer-facing platform.

Across that work I designed each stage of the interface, organized complex pathways, built prototypes, created multiple design systems, maintained variable color systems for dark and light modes, and helped the team continually improve user experience through iteration, research, feedback, and testing.

Outside of work I enjoy the mountains, riding bicycles, skateboarding, making music, and flying FPV drones.
